Who we reached

How these numbers are counted
  • People reached adds the attendance on local movement reports and the audience on staff activities. A person who comes to three events is counted three times, so this is not the number of different individuals.
  • Podcast downloads are not included in people reached. They are shown on their own.
  • Trainings combine movement-reported trainings and staff-led CHRADL trainings. If both log the same training it is counted twice, so check the table above.
  • Race, age and education figures come only from reports where the coordinator filled in the optional attendee details. They are estimates. The percentages are of the people counted, not of everyone who attended.
  • Counties reached counts each Mississippi county that has a movement report in the period, by the county on the report or the county of the reporting movement.

How voting works: a motion starts as a draft that only super admins see. Opening it shows it to the board and locks its wording. Board members vote and can change their vote until voting closes. While it is open nobody, including you, can see how anyone voted, only how many have voted. Closing it shows the totals to the board and locks every vote. Only people with the board role or the board flag can vote; staff super admins who are not board members cannot. Whether an electronic vote is valid for a given decision, and what counts as a quorum or a majority, depends on your bylaws and state law. Please confirm that with counsel before relying on an official vote.
